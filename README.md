# Home

This passion project is the documentation of my journey trying to build my own "home" and will be aggregation of my whole skillset, including but not limited to IT field. As for how exactly it should looks like in the end, I don't know and probably will never know. However, what's certain is the fact that it will be made with my genuine efforts, vision and hopefully hands, even if it means recreating the existing technologies or tools. It was heavily inspired by the comment I once saw: *"My journey began with 'Hello World' and grew into building my own world."*

Oh and personally for me, wherever is my sister, there is my "home". So, alternatively, this project could also be my attempt to ease my loneliness and longing for her, making every part of it have implicit or explicit connection to this wish of mine.

## Current Vision

3D game with a world limited to a house and maybe also backyard. The items inside the house will esentially be some kind of services recreated by me with minimum or, preferably, no dependencies at all for the purpose of making my life easier. For example, opening a chest should give an access to the service similar to Google Drive, or opening a notebook will give me access to a drawing software similar to Photoshop. Obviously, there should be 3d version of my sister who you can interact with. The game will be in an isolated environment, be it whole external device, virtual machine with its own custom made operating system, or both.
